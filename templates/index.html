From daylight solar to stored energy

Think of this as a cartoon model of a campus microgrid. Solar panels supply campus demand first. When solar generation exceeds immediate use, CarbonWise schedules the surplus to charge a battery instead of wasting it. When solar output falls or demand rises later, stored energy can help serve campus loads and reduce grid imports.

1Capture: rooftop solar generates electricity during daylight.
2Store: use surplus energy to charge the battery when there is room.
3Use later: discharge the battery when solar drops or the campus needs more power.
4Optimize: CarbonWise compares a small QAOA schedule with an exact classical baseline.
